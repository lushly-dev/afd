Four accessibility fixes. Under forced colours, a `violet-badge` with `appearance="subtle"` or `appearance="outline"` now takes the system colours, `CanvasText` on `Canvas` with a `ButtonText` border; its appearance's fill outranked the forced-colours rule, so a subtle badge in a dark contrast theme was white on a pale tint at about 3:1 (#1576). Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` the indeterminate `violet-progress` no longer slides: the fill spans the track and only its opacity eases. An animated `violet-skeleton` stops shimmering (#1583). A `violet-tab-panel` focused from the keyboard shows the theme's focus ring, in `Highlight` under forced colours; `violet-tabs` makes each panel a tab stop and nothing was drawn (#1584). The first `violet-alert` of a tone draws its own icon, not the registry's fallback cross: the alert registers its icons before it first renders, and a `violet-icon` now updates when its icon is registered after it rendered, so an app that registers icons late no longer needs to register them first (#1588). The workarounds for these (a `::part(badge)` forced-colours rule, hiding the bar or dropping `animated` under reduced motion, a `violet-tab-panel:focus-visible` outline, and registering the alert's icons by hand) can go.
