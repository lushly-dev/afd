# Violet system icons: the brief (Key)

Decided by the owner on 2026-11-25 (lushly-dev/afd#2567), from three candidates (Key, Slab and
Chamfer): **Key is the set.** Icons work in everyday UI first; the site's softer interface layer
(rounded keys, shadows) keeps the whole from reading sharp. Slab's mitre corners and solid blocks
went muddy at 16px; its look may return later for large display icons only, never system icons.

Key follows the site's interface layer, Refined: the icons live inside controls that are
rounded keys and wells, next to Inter.

- One even line, 1.75 on the 24 grid, round caps and round joins.
- Circles, arcs of circles and straight runs. Corners are rounded at 2 to 2.5 units,
  the grid's echo of Refined's 8px control radius.
- Closed, calm and neutral. A solid dot only where the object has one (a date, a full stop).
- Name it at 16px first.
