# AFD site icons: the brief

The control icons of afd.dev and afd.design, drawn in the style the owner chose on 2026-11-25 for
Violet's system icons (lushly-dev/afd#2567): its brief, in
`foundations/fast-af/packages/violet-elements/icons/sources/system/brief.md`, applies here.

- One even line, 1.75 on the 24 grid, round caps and round joins. The site shows them through a
  CSS mask (`.icon` in `site/css/manifesto-2.css`), so only the shape's alpha counts: at 18px,
  15px in the surface tabs and the agent intents, and 13px in the command rail and the narrow
  surface tabs.
- Circles, arcs of circles and straight runs; every corner and bend takes a 2 to 2.5-unit radius.
- Name it at 13px first, on a 1x screen.

They replace the Lucide icons the site used until then. MIT, by Lushly Dev, as the rest of the
repository. `close` is the same drawing as Violet's
`system/close`.
