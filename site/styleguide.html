Type ramp

Eight shared steps, plus sizes for the honesty statement and closing command. Archivo Black carries statements, Space Grotesk carries reading and the thin half of each statement, IBM Plex Mono carries labels and code, and Inter carries the interface: navigation, buttons, fields, tabs and chips (--font-ui). A size between two steps is a fraction of a step, never a new pixel value: a home page's poster line is four fifths of display, and a long tool name is 0.82 of it.

Controls

Things you press or type into are rounded (--radius-control; small marks --radius-mark), and panels a step more (--radius-panel). Edges come from surfaces and shadows, not outlines: filled buttons are keys, outline buttons are tonal keys, fields are wells and panels float. Posters, colour fields, figure chips and crop marks stay square.

Status

Preview. Say plainly what is released, what is not, and what it does not do yet. This block is where a page is most honest.

Running text

A last line of one word reads as a mistake. Paragraphs, list items and captions use text-wrap: pretty, and js/widows.js ties the last two words together in a browser that lacks it. Display type is exempt: there, one word on the last line is the design.

A hyphenated name goes in <span class="keep">, so FAST-AF never ends a line on FAST-.

Words

The pages argue that nothing should be claimed that can't be shown. The copy follows the same rule.

Do

  • Say what kind of thing it is in the first screen: "A design token engine."
  • Read every number on the page from a run. If the tool changes, the page changes with it.
  • Open a status with its ladder word, then where it is: "Preview, on npm".
  • Label a fact by where it came from, and say what the tool does not do yet.
  • Run the real tool in the page. Where part of it can't run in a browser, say which part.

Don't

  • Open a tool's page with a slogan and make the reader scroll to learn what it is.
  • Use a status word outside the ladder (Stable, Beta, Preview, In development, Internal).
  • Type a result into the page, or draw a demo that only looks live.
  • Show two things that look identical without saying why they are being shown.

Palette primitives

The raw palette (--afd-*). Section and component CSS reaches for semantic tokens first; primitives are for component-internal detail with no role of its own.

Space

A nine-step scale, plus --section-pad for supporting sections, --statement-pad for major story beats and --heading-gap below each headline.

Motion

Motion marks a moment; it never runs continuously behind reading.

Do

  • Light a statement word by word as it scrolls in (data-lit), on at most three statements per page.
  • Stamp a <mark> once its statement is fully lit.
  • Animate one-time entrances (the hero slash) with --motion-enter and --ease-out.
  • Give every animation a static state under prefers-reduced-motion.

Don't

  • Loop motion behind text people are reading.
  • Hide content with opacity alone; use hidden or inert.
  • Animate layout properties; stick to transform and opacity.
  • Add a second bright-red field, a second light-up per section, or drafting grids.
