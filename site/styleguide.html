Palette primitives

The raw palette (--afd-*). Section and component CSS reaches for semantic tokens first; primitives are for component-internal detail with no role of its own.

Space

A nine-step scale, plus --section-pad for every section's top and bottom and --heading-gap below each headline.

Motion

Motion marks a moment; it never runs continuously behind reading.

Do

  • Light a statement word by word as it scrolls in (data-lit), on at most three statements per page.
  • Stamp a <mark> once its statement is fully lit.
  • Animate one-time entrances (the hero slash) with --motion-enter and --ease-out.
  • Give every animation a static state under prefers-reduced-motion.

Don't

  • Loop motion behind text people are reading.
  • Hide content with opacity alone; use hidden or inert.
  • Animate layout properties; stick to transform and opacity.
  • Add a second bright-red field, a second light-up per section, or drafting grids.
