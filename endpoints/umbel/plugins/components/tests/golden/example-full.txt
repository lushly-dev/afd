[rule field.invalid-message authored]
> When invalid is set, say what is wrong in the hint slot; colour alone does not tell anyone.
[rule field.labelling authored]
> Give every field a label attribute; a nearby heading or a placeholder does not name it.
[rule popup.focus-return verified]
> Closing the popup returns focus to the control that opened it.
