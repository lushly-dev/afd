# Umbel bug sweep: load-sensitive locks and doctor port reuse

- Date: 2026-11-27
- Area: every other area
- Pull request: [#3007](https://github.com/lushly-dev/afd/pull/3007), on `t3/umbel-bug-sweep`, for [#2728](https://github.com/lushly-dev/afd/issues/2728) and [#2787](https://github.com/lushly-dev/afd/issues/2787) (refs [#1175](https://github.com/lushly-dev/afd/issues/1175))

Closed the remaining 5 s lock-timeout gaps in `umbel-store-embedded` tests ([#2728](https://github.com/lushly-dev/afd/issues/2728)). PRs [#2701](https://github.com/lushly-dev/afd/pull/2701) and [#2945](https://github.com/lushly-dev/afd/pull/2945) had raised `CHILD_LOCK_TIMEOUT_MS` to 60 s for the conformance factories and writer children, leaving `memory_keyfile::open`, `multiprocess::this_process_writes_while_two_others_do`, `multiprocess::a_second_process_waits_for_the_index_writer_then_is_busy`'s patient waiter, `lane_schema::two_handles_opening_a_version_1_store_migrate_it_once`, and `support::open_lane` on `StoreOptions::default()` (5 s). Running the full `umbel-store-embedded` suite at `--test-threads=16` alongside a concurrent `cargo build` reproduced `memory_keyfile::two_processes_write_keys_while_a_third_forgets` panicking at `memory_keyfile.rs:153` (`forget: Busy`). Those helpers now use `support::child_options()` (60 s), while `memory_keyfile::two_processes_write_keys_while_a_third_forgets` also pipes child stderr for diagnostics and gives its forget loop a 120 s deadline matching `memory_multiprocess`. Explicit short-timeout `Busy` tests (200 ms and 300 ms) and production `StoreOptions::default()` (5 s) are unchanged.

Hardened both load-sensitive integration tests in [#2787](https://github.com/lushly-dev/afd/issues/2787). `multi_process::two_hosts_interleave_two_hundred_mutations_without_a_gap` and `multi_process::many_umbel_processes_append_to_one_journal_with_contiguous_seq` now configure `[journal] lock_timeout_ms = 120000` instead of the 10 s default so 200 concurrent mutations plus a reader calling `event-list` (or 24 concurrent CLI processes) do not trip `WORKSPACE_LOCK_TIMEOUT` under machine load; `a_lock_held_past_the_timeout_fails_a_rebuild_and_only_logs_for_a_mutation` keeps its 100 ms timeout. In `person_page_gateway`, `PersonServed` now stores its task handle with an async `stop()` mirroring `support::hosted::Served::stop()`, and `the_doctor_checks_the_pages_own_listener` awaits `person.stop()` and `served.stop()` before the unreachable check and binds a fresh `127.0.0.1:0` listener (rewriting `umbel.toml` to that ephemeral port) for each impostor probe instead of re-binding a released port.

[#1175](https://github.com/lushly-dev/afd/issues/1175) did not reproduce on macOS at `815296823`: 30 consecutive runs of `cargo test --locked -p umbel --test it -- plugin_mounted:: --test-threads=16` passed (36/36 each, 1,080 test executions). Per its criteria, `#1175` stays open and its claim was released until a recurrence surfaces `ChildWatch` diagnostics.

Verified on macOS (Apple Silicon, Rust 1.100.0) on 2026-11-27: `cargo fmt --all --check`, `cargo clippy --locked -p umbel-store -p umbel-store-embedded -p umbel --all-targets -- -D warnings`, full `cargo test --no-fail-fast --locked -p umbel-store-embedded --test it -- --test-threads=16` (164/164 passed in 149.64 s), and 10 consecutive runs of `multi_process::` and `person_page_gateway::the_doctor_checks_the_pages_own_listener` (5/5 each).
