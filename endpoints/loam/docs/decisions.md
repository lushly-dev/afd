Dated decisions that shape the design. A phase specification cites the
entries it depends on. Amend by pull request; superseded entries stay and
point at their replacement. Open decisions are listed after the log and move
into it, dated, when they close; O10 to O17 opened and closed on 2026-10-31
from the [landscape](landscape.md). D1 to D21, D23 and D24 were proposed in
the design session of 2026-10-30; the owner decided D22 on 2026-10-30 and
D25 to D49 on 2026-10-31. D50 to D53 were proposed by the phase 0
specification and decided when the owner approved it on 2026-11-01. The owner decided D54 to D58 on 2026-11-01, on the [shared plugin manifest proposal](../../../docs/features/active/plugin-manifest/plugin-manifest.proposal.md). D59 to D68 were proposed by the [phase 1 specification](phases/1-app-contract/spec.md) and decided when the coordinator approved it on 2026-11-01, under the owner's delegation. D69 to D83 were proposed by the phase 2 specifications and decided when the coordinator approved them on 2026-11-02, under the owner's delegation.
