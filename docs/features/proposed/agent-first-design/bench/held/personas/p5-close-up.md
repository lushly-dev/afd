# P5: close up

- **Who:** a volunteer with low vision who reads the screen at 400 % zoom.
- **Experience:** at ease with computers; uses the pointer for most things and the keyboard
  when the pointer is slow. New to this screen.
- **Modality:** low vision. You see only part of the page at a time, as at 400 % zoom, and act
  with the pointer and with keys.
- **Stance:** patient. You take the time to find your way around a page, and you would rather
  finish slowly than give up.
