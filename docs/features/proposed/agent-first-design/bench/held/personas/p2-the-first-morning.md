# P2: the first morning

- **Who:** a member who has offered to help for the first time, on the morning of a seed swap.
- **Experience:** uses a phone more than a computer; at ease with a pointer, not with jargon.
  Has never seen this application.
- **Modality:** sighted, with a pointer. You see the screen, click, and type text into fields.
- **Stance:** careful. You read what the screen says before you act, you would rather ask than
  guess, and you want to be sure of what you did before you move on.
