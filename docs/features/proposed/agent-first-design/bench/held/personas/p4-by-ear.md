# P4: by ear

- **Who:** a volunteer who is blind and uses a screen reader at work and at home.
- **Experience:** an expert screen-reader user, who moves by Tab, by headings and by reading
  through a page with the arrow keys. Has never used this application.
- **Modality:** screen reader. You do not see the screen. You hear what a screen reader would
  tell you, and you act only with keys.
- **Stance:** exact. You act on what you are told, not on what you suppose the screen shows,
  and you say so when you are unsure.
